Winds – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Winds app ("the App") accesses, how it is used, and your choices. The App is a personal tool operated by Winds ("I", "me", "the developer").

1. Information the App Accesses

When a TikTok account authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App may receive:

  • Basic profile information that you approve on TikTok's authorization screen (for example, your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which allow the App to act on your behalf within the permissions you granted.
  • Videos and post details (such as caption and privacy setting) that are submitted to TikTok through the Content Posting API.

The App only requests the permissions needed to publish videos. It does not access your private messages, contacts, or payment information.

2. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to:

  • Authenticate the authorized TikTok account;
  • Upload and publish videos to that account; and
  • Check the status of those uploads.

3. Sharing of Information

I do not sell, rent, or trade your information. I do not share it with third parties, except as needed to operate the App through TikTok's own services, or when required by law.

4. Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored securely and are used only by the App. I take reasonable measures to protect them from unauthorized access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Tokens and related data are kept only for as long as the App needs them to operate. They are deleted when you revoke access or request deletion.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Revoke access: In the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions) and remove "Winds".
  • Request deletion: Email me at the address below and I will delete any data the App holds about your account.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Third-Party Services

The App relies on TikTok. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
